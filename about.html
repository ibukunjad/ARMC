About ARMC

Housing is personal.
Management should be dependable.

We bring structure to off-campus living, connecting the needs of property owners with the everyday realities of student life.

Our purpose

A housing management partner, from start to finish.

Academic Residences Management Company (ARMC) manages properties on behalf of landlords and provides structured tenancy to students.

Our role continues beyond finding a room or introducing a tenant. We coordinate rent administration, tenancy documentation, complaint handling and property oversight within the selected management plan.

Our mission is simple: protect the integrity of rent, reduce the stress of property ownership and make student housing more accountable.

The ARMC standard

Structure you can rely on.

01 / TRANSPARENCY

Know what you pay.

Approved rent and management fees are communicated clearly. No agent inflation or hidden markups.

02 / DOCUMENTATION

Put expectations in writing.

Formal owner and tenant agreements define responsibilities, payment terms and the management relationship.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Know who is responsible.

One team coordinates inquiries, complaints and escalation, with structured reporting to property owners.

How we work

One coordinated process.

Property onboarding

Agree on the property requirements, approved rent and one management plan.

Tenant administration

Source and screen tenants, prepare documentation and coordinate intake.

Ongoing management

Monitor compliance and handle complaints, with extra oversight under Premium.

Owner accountability

Provide structured reporting and advice throughout the rent cycle.

ARMC is an independent housing management company. Ask the team for registration details and documentation of any institutional relationship before relying on a claimed affiliation.
