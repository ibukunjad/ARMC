Living well, together.

What do I pay?

Annual rent is paid in advance at the approved rate, alongside the ₦10,000 annual tenant fee per room. Ask for written payment details before making any payment.

What are my responsibilities?

Use the premises for residential purposes, comply with the documented conduct rules and do not sublet without ARMC approval. Review the complete tenant agreement before accepting keys.

How do inspections work?

The tenancy terms provide for routine inspections with reasonable notice. Ask the team to explain the inspection arrangements for your property.

How do I report a problem?

Submit complaints and requests through ARMC’s official contact channels, including your lodge and room reference. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service first.

Does an inquiry mean I have booked a room?

No. Availability, screening, tenancy terms and payment must be confirmed with ARMC. This website does not take rent payments or issue booking confirmations.
