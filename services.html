Know the details.

Does ARMC increase the rent?

ARMC’s model protects the approved rent from agent inflation. The landlord management fee and the ₦10,000 annual tenant fee are stated separately.

When does the landlord pay?

There is no upfront landlord management fee. The annual management fee is payable at new rentage or renewal, when rent is earned, according to the signed agreement.

Can different rooms use different plans?

No. One management plan applies to each property. Plans cannot be mixed or customised.

Are repair costs included in Premium?

Premium includes maintenance coordination and oversight. Confirm repair budgets, payment responsibilities and approvals in the owner agreement. ARMC cannot execute structural repairs without owner consent.

Is there an early-adopter arrangement?

An early-adopter rate may be locked for an agreed period of 2–3 years. Ask ARMC to confirm eligibility, the exact duration and all conditions in writing before onboarding.
