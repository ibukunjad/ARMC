Agreements & privacy

Clear terms.
Shared responsibilities.

These summaries introduce ARMC’s management and tenancy arrangements. Request and read the complete agreement before signing, paying or accepting keys.

For property ownersFor tenantsWebsite privacy

Owner–management agreement

A clear mandate to manage.

  1. Management is exclusive for one year and renewable annually.
  2. ARMC controls tenant intake, rent receipts and compliance documentation.
  3. ARMC cannot execute structural repairs without owner consent.
  4. The owner remains responsible for maintaining the property’s structural integrity.
  5. Termination requires written notice and settlement of outstanding obligations.

The selected annual management plan and any early-adopter rate arrangement must be recorded in the signed agreement. This page is a summary, not the full contract.

Tenant agreement

Understand your tenancy.

  1. Annual rent is paid in advance at the approved rate.
  2. The premises are for residential use only.
  3. Subletting requires ARMC approval.
  4. ARMC conducts routine inspections with reasonable notice.
  5. All complaints are lodged through ARMC channels.
  6. The proposed tenancy terms state that acceptance of keys constitutes acceptance of the agreement. Read and clarify the complete agreement before accepting keys.

The executed agreement sets out the complete terms, subject to applicable law. Ask ARMC for a copy and clarification of anything you do not understand.

Website privacy

You control your inquiry.

The inquiry form prepares a message in your browser. It does not automatically submit information to ARMC or store it in a website database. Your entries are not saved by this website after you leave or reload the page.

If you choose email or WhatsApp, the prepared message is passed to that service. You must send it there yourself; that service’s own privacy terms then apply. Copying a message places the information on your device’s clipboard.

The form asks only for the information needed to discuss housing, property management or a concern. Do not include bank details, identity documents or other sensitive information. Before sending personal information, ask ARMC about its handling and retention practices.
